📚 How to play

  1. Two teams take turns guessing letters.
  2. A correct letter earns +1 point per guessed letter.
  3. Guess the whole word correctly for +3 points.
  4. Each incorrect guess adds a part to the classic hangman and switches teams. Six mistakes end a round.
  5. After solving a word, unlock the speaking question.
  6. Give +2 points for a full sentence and +1 for using the word correctly.
  7. Translate clues and revealed words to Danish when needed.
  8. Use Shuffle for a new word order. All 19 words are available offline.

Teacher tip: Have students discuss their answer for 15 seconds before speaking. Play 5–6 rounds for a 10–15-minute warm-up.
